About Engineering Calculators

Professional Engineering Calculation Tools

Engineering Calculators is a professional online platform providing free engineering calculation tools for electrical engineers, renewable energy professionals, technicians, students, and engineering enthusiasts worldwide.

The platform focuses on practical engineering applications including electrical system design, solar PV system sizing, cable selection, voltage drop analysis, energy calculations, and renewable energy solutions.

Our objective is to simplify complex engineering calculations by providing accurate, practical, and easy-to-use tools based on established engineering principles.


Our Mission

Our mission is to make engineering knowledge more accessible by creating reliable digital tools that help engineers perform calculations faster during design, analysis, and project planning.

We aim to support engineers, students, and technical professionals by providing practical resources that connect engineering theory with real-world applications.


Engineering Expertise

Engineering Calculators is developed with practical experience in electrical engineering and renewable energy systems.

  • Electrical system design and engineering analysis
  • Solar PV system design and performance evaluation
  • Grid-connected solar power projects
  • Cable sizing and voltage drop calculations
  • Renewable energy engineering solutions
  • Technical studies, feasibility analysis, and project support
