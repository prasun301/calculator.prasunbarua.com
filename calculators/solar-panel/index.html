How to Size a Solar PV System

Designing a reliable solar photovoltaic (PV) system starts with understanding your daily energy footprint. Utility bills usually show your monthly consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh). Dividing this by 30 gives your average daily kWh requirement.

The Sizing Formula

To account for environmental losses (such as inverter efficiency, wiring resistance, shading, and module temperature degradation), we apply an efficiency derate factor.

System Size (kW) = [Daily Energy (kWh) / Peak Sun Hours] / (Efficiency %)

Number of Panels = [System Size (kW) * 1000] / Panel Wattage (W)

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Peak Sun Hours?

Peak sun hours measure the intensity of sunlight. One peak sun hour equals 1,000 watts of photovoltaic power per square meter for one hour. It is different from standard daylight hours.
